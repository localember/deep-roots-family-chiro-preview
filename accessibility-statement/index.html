Accessibility Statement

Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Last Updated: January 1, 2025

Deep Roots Family Chiropractic is committed to making this website accessible to as many people as possible, including people with disabilities. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA where it is reasonably possible to do so.

Measures we take

We work to provide clear headings, readable contrast, text alternatives for meaningful images, keyboard-accessible navigation, and pages that can be resized without loss of essential content. This Site is a design preview, and we will continue to improve accessibility as the live site is built.

Known limits

Some older files or third-party content may not meet the same standard. If you find a barrier, please tell us so we can fix it.

Assistance

If you have trouble using this website, or if you need information in a different format, contact Deep Roots Family Chiropractic at Braden@deeprootsfamilychiro.com. We will work with you to provide the information another way.
