Discover Gonstead Chiropractic Care

The Gonstead system is a rigorous five-part analysis used to find the specific area in the spine that is interfering with a patient's health, then adjust only what needs it.

X-rays

Full-spine films, where appropriate, to see alignment, structure, and the discs.

Palpation

Hands-on checks of the spine, both still and in motion, to locate the segment that needs care.

Instrumentation

A dual-probe instrument compares temperature along both sides of the spine to help find inflammation.

Visualization

How you stand, move, and carry your posture, from the moment you walk in.

Case Management

Your history, what you feel, and a clear plan based on the findings of the analysis.

Meet Braden Beyer

Deep Roots Family Chiropractic

“The doctor of the future will give no medication, but will interest his patients in the care of the human frame, diet and in the cause and prevention of disease.” - Thomas Edison

Growing up Braden always wanted to be an engineer like his dad, solving problems and figuring out how things worked. However, in 2013 he had several concussions and soon had daily headaches. Amongst all the professionals he saw, chiropractic it was gave him his life back. This began his personal journey of pursuing chiropractic and learning how the body was designed to be healthy. Years later he was introduced to the Gonstead work, a rigorous five-part system that allows the chiropractor to find the specific area in the spine that is interfering with the patient’s health. He loved the systematic approach and a system that would allow his to offer the best care possible for his patients.

He’s excited to be opening Deep Roots Family Chiropractic in Fort Wayne to help bring better health to more families in his community. He’ll be earning his Doctor of Chiropractic degree from Palmer College of Chiropractic in Davenport, Iowa, the founding college of the chiropractic profession. He’s also completed his Gonstead certification through the Gonstead Methodology Institute, including more than 100 hours of advanced training where he continues to attend seminars on a regular basis. He holds his Bachelor of Science with a minor in Spanish from Purdue University, where he graduated with honors.

While at Palmer, Braden Beyer joined the Troxell Intern Program, a competitive Gonstead internship based out of a local chiropractic office in the Quad Cities. He served as President of the program, taught workshops and classes to fellow chiropractic students, and helped with new patient exams and consults for the office he interned at. Before Palmer, he spent five years working in chiropractic offices in Lafayette and Fort Wayne, serving patients and growing in his knowledge of running an office.

Braden Beyer's professional skills include:

  • Precise full-spine analysis using x-ray, Delta-T instrumentation, and hands-on palpation
  • Specific pediatric and prenatal training in the Gonstead system, the Webster approach, and craniosacral therapy.
  • Over 250 hours of training in the Gonstead work (hands-on technique, spinal analysis skills, case management, and more)
  • Clear, thorough new-patient exams and consultations
  • Spanish-language care for Spanish-speaking families

Outside the office, Braden enjoys being outside, hiking and playing music, and he has served as a worship leader and youth group leader at Headwaters Church in Fort Wayne, IN. Most of all, he loves spending time with his wife and son, Honor and Beren.
